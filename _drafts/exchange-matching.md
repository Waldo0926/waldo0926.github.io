When planning Monash Abroad courses, I learned not to treat a similar course title as evidence of credit equivalence.

Titles are search clues. Course outlines, assessment topics, credit points, level and the teaching period are the stronger evidence. A unit that exists but isn't offered during the exchange semester is no use to the plan.

I want to distinguish candidates backed by public information, cases discussed with a faculty and matches with formal written approval. An old mapping must be rechecked when offerings change.

This draft excludes private application emails, student identifiers, grades and unpublished approval records. It will become a personal account only after I verify what can be shared.
