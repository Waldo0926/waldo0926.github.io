{%- comment -%} Puts a around Chinese punctuation so a long title breaks there instead of in the middle of a word. Chinese has no spaces, so by default a browser may break between any two characters, which splits 工具 across two lines. CSS has no portable fix: word-break: auto-phrase only works for Japanese in Chrome and is in neither Firefox nor Safari. Closing marks get the breakpoint after them, opening marks before them, so a line never ends on an opening bracket or quote. Used together with the word-break: keep-all rule in assets/style.css, which makes these the only breakpoints. A stretch of text with no punctuation that still does not fit falls back to overflow-wrap and breaks by character, which is what the browser did before. Usage: {% include zh-wrap.html text=page.title %} {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign out = include.text -%} {%- assign after = "，|、|：|；|。|？|！|）|》|」|』|”|’|…" | split: "|" -%} {%- for mark in after -%} {%- capture broken -%}{{ mark }}{%- endcapture -%} {%- assign out = out | replace: mark, broken -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign before = "（|《|「|『|“|‘" | split: "|" -%} {%- for mark in before -%} {%- capture broken -%}{{ mark }}{%- endcapture -%} {%- assign out = out | replace: mark, broken -%} {%- endfor -%} {{- out -}}
