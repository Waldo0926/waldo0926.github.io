I'm Shuoxun Wen (温硕勋), studying Computer Science (Algorithms and Software) at Monash University. I'm looking for backend and AI application internships.

Most of what I build is for Monash students, and I run it myself: a VPS with Docker, nginx, automated deploys and a public [status page](https://waldo0926.github.io/uptime/). This blog is where I write down how those projects work and what went wrong along the way.

## Projects

- [Monash Hub](https://monashhub.secureview.tech): unit and degree information, a course map, a WAM calculator and the official student guides, in Chinese, English, Japanese and Korean. ([code](https://github.com/Waldo0926/monash-hub))
- [SecureView](https://github.com/Waldo0926/SecureView): a secure file sharing platform where the browser encrypts every file. Our final year project; I wrote 182 of the 203 merged pull requests.
- [Monash Study Kit](https://github.com/Waldo0926/monash-study-kit): lets Claude and other MCP clients read your own Moodle and Ed, with full-text search of course files. On PyPI.
- [Student Q&A bot](https://github.com/Waldo0926/mumguide-wechat-bot): answers students and parents on WeChat from rules, with a model that may only quote. The checking code is its own package, [quotegate](https://github.com/Waldo0926/quotegate).
- [Attendance Helper](https://github.com/Waldo0926/monash-attendance-reminder): a Chrome extension that finds attendance codes and submits only the classes you confirm.
- [Ed Web](https://github.com/Waldo0926/ed-web): reads and searches an Ed forum in the browser, with no backend.

Guides for the student tools are at [docs.secureview.tech](https://docs.secureview.tech). Everything else is on [GitHub](https://github.com/Waldo0926).
