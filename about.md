I'm Shuoxun Wen (温硕勋), studying Computer Science (Algorithms and Software) at Monash University.

A lot of what I build is for Monash students, but I also work on algorithms, data and Linux/network infrastructure. For the [complete public project archive](/projects/), see the separate projects page. I run my public sites myself: a VPS with Docker, nginx, automated deploys and a public [status page](https://waldo0926.github.io/uptime/). This blog is where I write down how those projects work and what went wrong along the way.

## Projects

- [Monash Hub](https://monashhub.secureview.tech): unit and degree information, a course map, a WAM calculator, Malaysia tuition fees, key dates and the official student guides, in English and Chinese. ([code](https://github.com/Waldo0926/monash-hub))
- [SecureView](https://secureview.tech): a secure file sharing platform where the browser encrypts every file. Our final year project, with work on testing, deployment and documentation. ([code](https://github.com/jsonrar/fyp-secure-file-platform))
- [Monash Study Kit](https://github.com/Waldo0926/monash-study-kit): [1.11.2 is on PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/monash-study-kit/1.11.2/). Export Moodle Pages, Books, assignment descriptions and attachments, Ed Lessons and course-linked Google files as complete course packages, with PDF / Markdown, isolated profiles and materials-only mode. AI / MCP access, full-text search and optional mail and attendance integrations are also available. See the [course materials guide](https://docs.secureview.tech/study-kit/materials/).
- [Student Q&A bot](https://github.com/Waldo0926/mumguide-wechat-bot): answers students and parents on WeChat from rules, with a model that may only quote. The checking code is its own package, [quotegate](https://github.com/Waldo0926/quotegate).
- [Attendance Helper](https://github.com/Waldo0926/monash-attendance-reminder): a Chrome extension that finds attendance codes and submits only the classes you confirm. On the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/monash-attendance-helper/mnbkcmgkjmfngcifndchaidndhdfafcd).
- [Ed Web](https://github.com/Waldo0926/ed-web): reads and searches an Ed forum in the browser, with no backend.

Guides for student tools are at [docs.secureview.tech](https://docs.secureview.tech). Older coursework and experiments are in the [project archive](/projects/); their READMEs explain what was rebuilt and what is no longer deployed.
