About

I’m Shuoxun Wen (温硕勋), studying Computer Science (Algorithms and Software) at Monash University.

Most of what I build is for Monash students, and I run it myself: a VPS with Docker, nginx, automated deploys and a public status page. This blog is where I write down how those projects work and what went wrong along the way.

Projects

  • Monash Hub: unit and degree information, a course map, a WAM calculator, Malaysia tuition fees, key dates and the official student guides, in English and Chinese. (code)
  • SecureView: a secure file sharing platform where the browser encrypts every file. Our final year project; I wrote 187 of the 212 merged pull requests.
  • Monash Study Kit: lets Claude and other MCP clients read your own Moodle and Ed, with full-text search of course files. Since 1.4 ChatGPT and Claude on the web can use it too, through a remote mode you host yourself, and since 1.5 it can also read your school mail and attendance, with every mail action or check-in waiting for your confirmation. On PyPI.
  • Student Q&A bot: answers students and parents on WeChat from rules, with a model that may only quote. The checking code is its own package, quotegate.
  • Attendance Helper: a Chrome extension that finds attendance codes and submits only the classes you confirm. On the Chrome Web Store.
  • Ed Web: reads and searches an Ed forum in the browser, with no backend.

Guides for the student tools are at docs.secureview.tech. Everything else is on GitHub.
